Project Hail Marry

The best movie I've seen lately was Project Hail Marry. It was a light hearted science fiction film with star Ryan Gosling. Ryan Gosling is a science teacher that got sent into space to try to solve a world ending issue. He and an alien life become close as they both had the same wold ending problem. They then work together to save their planets


This was by far my favorite movie of the year. Well shot, written and directed. Ryan Gosling really shined in this role, as i belive it was one of his best roles to date.
